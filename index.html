About

I work at the intersection of public international law, the law of the sea, climate change and human rights.

I am an international law scholar and attorney. I completed my PhD in International Law at Maastricht University in 2026, with a thesis on sovereignty over natural resources in the context of sea-level rise. My academic work also draws on earlier research at the University of Amsterdam and an LL.M. in Public International Law from Leiden University.

Research

Sea-level rise & sovereignty

Permanent sovereignty over natural resources, changing maritime zones and the continuity of resource sovereignty.

Climate change & international law

International legal responses to climate change, including questions of fairness, territory and human mobility.

Law of the sea

Maritime boundaries, shifting coastlines and legal stability in a changing physical environment.

International criminal law

Accountability, international courts and the relationship between international criminal law and contemporary conflicts.
